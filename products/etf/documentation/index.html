Bangladesh

The BNQF vocabulary follows Section 5.1 and Sections 6.1–6.10 of the supplied English translation of the Bangladesh National Qualifications Framework (BNQF) Policy, 2026. It represents Levels 1–10, covering skills, technical and vocational, school and madrasah, and higher education qualifications. Pre-vocational education precedes Level 1 and is not assigned an additional numbered level. National Skills Certificates 1–6 are included within the corresponding BNQF levels rather than represented as a separate framework.

Qualification examples are summaries, not the full learning-outcome descriptors. Placement at the same BNQF level does not establish equivalence between qualifications; the policy explicitly distinguishes the four-year diploma from NSC 6. No mapping to EQF levels is asserted. The supplied document is labelled as a draft English translation; Section 15 states that the Bangla text prevails if the texts differ.
